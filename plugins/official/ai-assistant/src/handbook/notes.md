## Recipe: notes from a source
1. `vd heading level=1` with the source's subject.
2. Per main idea: `vd heading level=2` and a `vd text` of short pointers in your own words. Keep facts that are in the source only.
3. A `vd diagram` when the source describes a process, a sequence or a structure.
4. `vd note` with open questions or follow-ups when the source raises them.
